# Source note — IEEE NMDC 2021 Presentation Preparation Guidelines

URL: https://ieeenmdc.org/past-conferences/nmdc-2021/call-for-papers/presentation-preparation-guidelines-and-requirements/
Accessed: 2026-10-21
Type: conference author guideline (not peer-reviewed research)

## Quoted guidelines relevant to this project

> "Use Calibri or a similar sans serif font. Do not use font sizes smaller than 24 point."

> "For pages with text only, use no more than 30 words and no more than 6 lines, per slide."

> "High contrast is important. Use black or dark colored fonts on a white background."

> "Leave 1/2 inch or 1 cm margin on all four sides."

## Why we keep it

It is a concrete, citable *convention* for (a) a minimum font size and (b) a text-density
limit per slide. It is NOT empirical evidence: no study is cited, and the numbers differ
across conferences. We use it to show that our thresholds are not arbitrary, while
stating explicitly that the field lacks validated values.
