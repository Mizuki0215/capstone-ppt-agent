## What it appears to be

A study examining whether medical-school lecture slides follow Mayer's Cognitive Theory of
Multimedia Learning (CTML) — i.e. whether real teaching decks violate principles such as
coherence (exclude irrelevant material), redundancy (avoid duplicating narration as text)
and signalling.

## Why we keep it

It is the closest thing to evidence that "too much text on slides" is a real and widespread
problem in practice, rather than a hypothetical. Before citing, verify:
  - author, year, venue
  - sample size and coding protocol
  - whether the outcome is CTML adherence, learning gain, or both
